## What the game is now: the V1 session, two ways in

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-15-v1-session-design.md`. Two entry points, both real runs at the full threat:

- **The bare page** (`index.html`) plays start to finish. It opens on **the welcome** (`2026-10-05-welcome-settlement-guide`, `src/fx/start-gate.js`): the A6 settlement pocket guide over the loading landing, its keywords turning their units in wireframe, Isao's WELCOME! on the first gesture and Ad Astra Per Aspera on START A NEW PLANET. Then **the landing itself** (`2026-09-25-arrival-landing`, `src/fx/arrival.js`): about 10 s at every story start (`?story=0` is the same page); the SH02 comes down, Isao climbs out and the camera cuts to his face for his three lines, the last about cannibalizing the rocket for the terraforming, while the rocket is swapped for its salvage and the foundry behind the camera (`2026-09-25-story-zero-and-the-cannibalize-line`). Then **sector 0, THE FOUNDATION** (`2026-09-24-sector-zero-built`): the foundry recycles the rocket, Isao works the AFR-01, prints the Rotor, the gate and walls and the Quiver, then starts the Stålheart, one 75 s print with a `STÅLHEART %` readout. Its construction is defended from the seats: the fifty on the Rotor, the Quiver's two hard cores, then construction waves every 11 s with the gunship arriving from orbit for a free pass. There is no hull until the Stålheart stands; the first MÖRK rolls out of it (129 s on a bare page, the landing included), the handover follows (151 s) and sector 1 opens at about 283 s, once the expedition is home or 90 s have passed (`--pacing`, 2026-09-25: the landing put the whole session about 5 s later). Isao then keeps printing the solar array, the bays, HUGIN, the radar and the assembly line through his travel-and-print loop; each printed step switches on a perk (`src/content/base-programme.js`, `src/domain/build-programme.js`, `src/fx/base-print.js`, `src/fx/hull-issue.js`). Explicit `stage=N` and `story=N` links stay static and sparse.
- **The tutorial card** (`2026-09-25-tutorial-chapters`, `src/fx/tutorial-card.js`): from the landing until sector 1 the opening's chrome says where the run is, TUTORIAL n/6 (LANDING, ROTOR, FIRST WAVE, QUIVER, STÅLHEART, EXPEDITION), with NEXT to the next chapter's start and SKIP ALL. Every chapter is a URL, `index.html?skip=<chapter>#td` (`rotor`, `wave`, `quiver`, `stalheart`, `expedition`, and `sector-1` past the last): a growing base built at boot as a run has it there, the programme's printed steps, the sentries, a print under way and the foundry's cut sections (`STORY_CHAPTERS` in `src/content/story-defaults.js`, `src/fx/story-entry.js`). The drawer's QUIVER and STUDY jumps are two of them.
- **SKIP ALL** (SKIP TUTORIAL, `index.html?skip=defence#td`): a run at the finished base, past the handover, with the Relay and the Mortar earned, opening at sector 1 (since 2026-10-01; `?skip=defence&sector=N` opens further on, `sector=6` on the canyon again, and the drawer's BACK DOOR jump is `sector=7`); the colony's four steps still print in play (2026-10-01 late) (`src/core/story-route.js`, `STORY_SKIP` in `src/content/story-defaults.js`).
- **Sectors are the loop past the handover** (`src/content/sectors.js`, `src/domain/sectors.js`, `src/fx/sector-run.js`): two breaches per sector, **their waves on a clock** (`2026-09-24-sector-waves-on-a-clock`): a pulse every 12-16 s whatever is alive, waves stacking when the player falls behind, bounded by a frame budget that follows the machine (the crowd cap, `2026-10-05-twenty-fourth-notes-lance-on-barrel-gunship-reach-crowd-cap`) and partly packed into squads of five (`2026-10-05-squads-five-bodies-to-an-entity`); breaches open under the brief card on a near ring (45 hops, ten cells off the base's rim) and bodies march at 1.5.
