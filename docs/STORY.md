# Story planet and arrival

Open `labs.html#story`. The lab generates the story planet on load: the pinned grid kernel at 16,000 points (71,314 cells), radius 753 m at 10 m per cell, with the defense map's lane density. Generation takes about four seconds on the Mac Mini; the HUD reports the time.

The clearing is at the north pole, 150 m in radius. Every cell inside is open floor. The lane mouths on its rim are found as runs of open cells just outside the radius; one mouth of at most two cells stays open, the rest are sealed to rock. The base frame is rotated so the open mouth lies on the frame's -Z axis, where the armored gate will seat.

The lattice stays natural by default (`terraces: false`); islands level their own plots with the slab's skirt. The concentric terrace profile remains available in `src/core/terrace-profile.js` when a plate design needs it.

**Arrival.** L lands the SH02 on the landing island, 60 m from the pole. The rocket is already burning and falling from 900 m when the 4 s orbit shot opens; the descent eases into touchdown at 12 s, legs deploying at 40 m, then the authored shock with dust and scorch, a 2 s settle, the top door, and Isao rising over seven seconds from deep inside the hull tube, past the rim, before drifting clear. The pole is kept for the Stalheart. K skips to the landed state, R resets the camera. Drag to orbit and wheel to zoom at any time; touching the camera leaves the rail. The timeline is `src/domain/landing-sequence.js` and is Node-tested; the clips are played as authored and never blended.

Ownership: `src/content/story-defaults.js` (recipe and tunables), `src/core/terrace-profile.js`, `src/domain/story-planet.js`, `src/domain/landing-sequence.js`, `src/fx/launch-plume.js`, `src/labs/story-planet-mesh.js`, `src/labs/story-landing.js`, `src/labs/story-tab.js`. No module imports the game controller. The SH02 is pinned in `docs/sh-rocket-assets.lock.json`; credits in ATTRIBUTIONS.md.

**On a phone.** Start the dev server with `node scripts/serve.mjs --host 0.0.0.0` and open `http://<mac-lan-ip>:8155/index.html?story=4#td` on the same Wi-Fi, or use the published site `https://kai-denrei.github.io/stalheart/index.html?story=4#td` once main has deployed.

Validation: `test/terrace-profile.mjs`, `test/story-planet.mjs`, `test/landing-sequence.mjs`; `node scripts/browser-test.mjs --story` drives the real page through rest, descent, touchdown, live play and skip with screenshots.

**Stages.** Digits 0 to 7 or the stage buttons show the base as it grows: 0 empty pole, 1 landing, 2 the landing island, 3 solar island and one Rotor, 4 the armored gate on the open mouth with six wall segments each side, 5 the HUGIN tower and catcher (booster hidden), 6 the Stalheart at the pole, 7 the assembly line and the single 18 m antenna on its radar island. `src/content/base-layout.js` holds the plots and stages; `src/domain/base-plan.js` turns them into placements, tested for overlap, monotonic stages, the gate on the mouth and corner sag under the slab's 1.2 m skirt. Every island and structure is tangent to the sphere at its own centre. `src/fx/story-base.js` draws the plan for both the lab and the game: one instanced slab per island with the 4 m grid drawn in the shader, instanced walls, the gate, and the landmark models with their authored cycles. O frames an overview.

**The story is the game's front door.** A bare `index.html` is the story world at stage 1; the legacy campaign, rescue and raid name themselves in the query (`classic=1`, `mission=`), as do the practice mode and the automated probes (`src/core/story-route.js`). The burger lists story, arrival (the lab playing the landing), classic, rescue and raid; leaving a mode drops its switches. In the story, `?cine=1` opens the arrival lab rather than the legacy cold open. The legacy gantry heart and containers exist only in classic, rescue and raid.

**Play here.** P, or the button, opens the actual game on this planet through the deep link `index.html?story=N#td` (short for `world=story&stage=N&threat=0.35&heart=none&cine=0`), with the lab's current stage, so the game shows the space as it is: at stage 1 only the rocket stands on natural ground. `heart=none` keeps the game's own heart off the pole; the story base places the Terraformer 3000 there at stage 6. `world=story` routes the controller's map through `src/domain/world-recipe.js`, which returns the story planet with the heart at the pole cell and 4 m walls; `threat` multiplies wave size (0.1 to 4). The default world is unchanged. The game's own sector reveal, gate placement and camp still apply; islands, the story build stages and the encounter beats are not in the game yet.

**Story-mode rules in the game.** With `world=story` the controller starts clean past the landing: `heart=none` draws nothing at the pole, so the Terraformer 3000 placed by the story base is the Stalheart; no portals are seeded and the wave clock never arms; the camp containers, the station ring and the Terraformer's hull and container yard are skipped. Wall cells from the base plan are rock to the pathfinder and the tank; the gate's cell stays open and the gate plays its authored clip open when the tank comes within 22 m, closing again behind it. Enemies do not exist yet in this world, so "non-core enemies cannot damage walls" is a rule for the encounter sub-project, where the enemy path field will also exclude the gate cell while it is closed.

**Stages from the menu.** In the story, the burger shows a stage strip under the tab bar: 0 to 7 plus the arrival cinematic, each a click away, no URL editing.

**One muzzle for every mode.** The game now draws a sentry's muzzle flash from the package's muzzle recipe, tune and colours, exactly as the Sentry / Impact lab does, scaled from lab metres onto the sphere; the old dot burst that threw sparks three cells wide is gone. In the lab, round projectiles fly nose first and their calibre follows the tracer size squared, so a Rotor round is under half a Needle's.

**Rounds stop at rock.** A sentry round's range is capped at the first rock it would fly into (its own parapet excepted), and it lands with a ring and a spark burst there instead of passing through.

**Encounter, the opening beats.** Isao's faces play over the landing in the game too: "Rough landing!" angry, then "So much to build!" delighted, as briefs. In the lab one medium-close shot on the nose is held from the door onward: the door opens, Isao rises out angry, then turns delighted, with a hold on the smile; no zoom, and a comms line shows the words. After the Rotor stands on a gated world: a tremor contact pulses on the radar far out past the tunnel, with Isao's line; the ground opens twenty-eight lane cells out, far enough that investigating it later is a tank trip, with the breach effect and the game's own orbit shot; the first easy enemies emerge from it and walk up the lane; the Rotor holds fire on its own (story sentries have no auto-targeting until the override), so they pile up against the gate; only then does Isao say the sentries are not ready for auto-targeting and the manual override hands the Rotor over. On the fifth kill Isao notes they are talking to each other and starts a study of the pattern (a seed for a later beat on reverse-engineering the alien language with machine learning; visuals to come from the operator's synthetic-learningx58 CRT neural-net demos); on the tenth, that their biomass will do as a printing ingredient. Without a gate the override comes straight after the print.

**Encounter, beats one to three.** In the story world at stage 1 or later, `src/domain/story-beats.js` runs Isao's script: two and a half seconds after the landing it grants the Rotor's cost and places a real build order on the wall beside the tunnel mouth: the rock cell touching the forward lane cell, chosen to the side, so the sentry stands on high ground the way every sentry will in this mode (there is no slab under it), which the platform hook registers as a floor socket so the placement rule accepts it and the tower mounts at ground level. Isao spawns at the landing island, flies to the socket and prints the Rotor with the game's own travel-and-print loop. The static Rotor model is skipped in the game, and the game's own opening garrison is skipped too, so the wall Rotor is the first print. A second and a half after it stands, the game enters sentry control on that Rotor: a 3.2 s shot turns three quarters of the way round the tower and settles behind it looking down the lane, then the optic takes over. Moving and shooting are separate: on a computer a click locks the mouse and the mouse aims, Space fires; on the phone a drag aims, the tank pad's side zones turn the mount and its fire button holds the trigger. The Rotor's barrel cluster winds up while firing and down after. Views follow the tank's keys: 1 map, 2 PoV with the barrels in the bottom of the frame, 3 third person behind and above the turret. The aim ray always starts at the optic on the mount; only the camera eye moves. The practice chrome (weapon swaps, posts, bench) is hidden in the story. Once a gate stands (stage 4), phage walk up the lane from six cells out, one every 2.5 s, never more than eight alive and twenty in all; the closed gate's cell is a wall to them, so they pile up outside where the Rotor can reach them. Without a gate no fodder comes. The piloted sentry fires at 2.5x its auto cadence with 5x damage per round (`STORY_PILOT`), so a phage drops in two hits, and the Rotor's tracer was promoted through the package path to a 6 px head with seven ghost points (`rotor-tracer-dense`). No field manual and no tutorial run in the story world; both will be rebuilt once the pieces exist. Isao's briefs still carry the old fiction and are the next text to rewrite for the new world.

**Z-fighting.** Structures ride 6 cm above their slab and the slab material carries a polygon offset, so coplanar floors (the assembly line's plate, the solar generator's base) no longer flicker against the slab.

**Kit.** Base pieces come from the upstream base kit: the game-ready base kit (walls D0-D3, corner, vehicle gate, 4 m tile) and the 28-triangle scalable island slab are pinned in `docs/base-kit-assets.lock.json` under `assets/models/kit/`.

**Sound.** The owner-provided rocket thrust sample loops under the descent, scaled by plume intensity, and cuts at touchdown. The tank's spool-up plays for leg deployment and the door, spool-down for the landing. Cues are pinned in `docs/rocket-audio.lock.json`. The armored gate plays karyoku's hydraulics when it starts opening and its slam when it has closed (`docs/story-audio.lock.json`), in the lab and in the game. Six plume quads hang under the skirt, each breathing on its own cadence.
