# The terminal and the bestiary (design, 2026-10-14)

Owner, 2026-10-14: "re-vamp the Units section; Vivarium, Bestiary, Compendium, we will find it a better name and 1) display all the unlocked units in their full glory, enemies at high resolution with smooth animations displaying their movement patterns. 2) each friendly unit as a wireframe (default) and toggleable full detail with animation diorama, each with a small lore-friendly explanation as to what it does, 3) each sentry with wireframe, toggleable full detailed version with shooting animations against pearl-core enemies. All this as if was accessed from a Terminal as a screen-within-a-screen, with HUD elements and a border and CRT like display effects."

Decided in the brainstorm (the owner's answers): one module serves the game and the lab (the lab with everything unlocked); three pieces, the enemies first (this spec), the friendly units and the sentries as later pieces that become pages of the same terminal; opened from the base console and a key, no HUD element ("we keep the screen hud to minimal"); unlocked is both: this run's contacts in full, earlier runs' entries as silhouettes with a "no recent reading" note; one entry per Pearl tier (eight) plus the kit creatures (the Cutis-Maku, the Reeds, the Nih-Dairia) as entries of their own once met; each stage a small pack (three or six) hunting a lure, the hard-cored tiers with a warning that they cannot be rammed; the text in Isao's voice, dry, slightly humorous, science-fiction references here and there, not overdone. Approach chosen: a stage scene on a render target under a terminal frame, one WebGL context, the game paused under it (the alternatives, the frame over the live world and a page of its own, are in the brainstorm's record). The working name is THE BESTIARY until the owner finds a better one; the code calls the screen the terminal and the page the bestiary so the name can change in content alone.

The controller (`src/td-tab.js`) wires: the key, the console's prompt, the pause, the contact log and the sightings store, the frame's `step`/`render` while open (the game's own frame is not drawn under it: the overlay is opaque black behind the frame, so the only render while open is the stage's). The line budget holds (6,120 of 6,400): the wiring is a handful of lines against a factory literal; the harness gets `bestiary: { open, close, state }`.

## 2. Opening and closing

- **The key:** `B` (free; the executor verifies against every key handler). Outside the opening and the ending, any time the hull is the player's: the game pauses (`pause.set(true)`, as `gunship-briefing.js openPaused` does) and the terminal opens over it; closing restores the pause state it found. During a seat (the gunship, SOL's), a shot or a modal the key does nothing.
- **The console:** under the Stålheart's gantry (the hull inside the structure's footprint, as `src/fx/story-base.js solidAt`'s footprint tells it; the hull drives under the Stålheart by the owner's rule) a one-line prompt sits at the bottom of the view, the controls card's style: `TERMINAL · B`. It is the only HUD trace and it is gone the moment the hull leaves the footprint. The first time a run passes under it with the terminal never opened, Isao says one line (section 6, `console_first`), through `isaoSpeak` with a new trigger key, no recording yet (text only, as the unrecorded lines are handled today).
- **The lab:** `labs.html#units` gains a BESTIARY button in its header that opens the same terminal over the units tab, every entry `full`, the stage running; the carousel stays for models.

## 3. Unlocked: the roster's state

- **This run's contacts:** `src/fx/contact-card.js` keeps `root.__contacts.log` (the types met in order). The controller hands `contacts()` → that log.
- **Sightings across runs:** a storage record `bestiary` (through `src/storage.js`'s wrapper, under its prefix) holding `{ [entryId]: { first: ISO date, runs: n, last: ISO date } }`, written when a run meets an entry for the first time in that run (the contact card's `showContact` is the one place a kind rises first: it calls a `sightings.note(entryId)` the controller gives it). `exportRecords`/`importRecords` carry it like the other records.
- **State per entry** (`src/domain/bestiary.js entryState`): met this run → `full` (the stage runs, the card is complete); met in an earlier run only → `silhouette` (the stage draws the pack as dark silhouettes, flat shaded, the card's text replaced by the note `NO RECENT READING · last logged <date>` and the data lines kept); never → `unseen` (listed as `— — —` with the tier's swatch dimmed, not selectable). The lab's host says every entry is `full`.
- A type maps to its tier through `PEARL_OF` (`src/content/pearl-enemies.js`); the dot-cloud run (no `?creatures=pearl`) logs the same types, so the bestiary fills the same way.

## 4. The stage

- The entry's pack: `pack` of 3 by default, Enter/Space toggles 6 (the content's `packSizes: [3, 6]`), seated round the lure's start on the bent disc, each body of the entry's smallest type's size (`pearlBodies` as the game builds them) so the pack reads as the game's bodies, at the kit's HIGH level forced with the cap at the pack's size; the membrane's lumps, the breath, the shadow and the core all on (the "full glory").
- The lure: a marker that moves on a closed loop (a slow lemniscate or circle, `lure: { radius, period }` in content), the pack's `track` target following it; the bodies hop after it, reach with their stalks, seek, turn; they never catch it (the lure keeps `ahead` of the nearest body: the domain's `lurePoint(t, nearest)` rule).
- The camera: a slow orbit (`orbit: { radius, height, period }`) around the disc's centre, the planet bend at the game's radius so the bodies' shaders bend as in play.
- Silhouette state: the family's materials replaced by a flat dark material (the kit's `uDebug`/palette uniforms if they allow it, else an override material on the group), the lure off.
- The stage renders to a target of the inner box's size at the device pixel ratio, then through the CRT pass (colour kept: the tier's colour inside is the point) onto the frame's quad. Cost: one small scene with 3–6 bodies, no measurable load against a paused game.

## 5. The entry's card

Right of the stage, in the terminal's type (the game's monospace HUD face), top to bottom:
- `ENTRY 04 / 11 · ORANGE TIER` (the index and the tier), the name line (`WAVE SATURN` for the type met, under the tier's name), the swatch.
- The data lines, from `ENEMY_SPEC` and the tier table, for every type the tier stands for: `TYPE · HIT POINTS · SPEED · SIZE · RAM` (RAM: `goes under the treads` or `SOLID CORE`), and `MET: run 3 · first 2026-10-14`.
- For a cored tier, the warning line in the content's warning style (amber, blinking once on open): `⚠ SOLID CORE · CANNOT BE RAMMED · the hull is stopped and pushed back`.
- The text: Isao's entry (section 6), three to five lines.
- The footer: `↑↓ ENTRIES · ⏎ PACK 3/6 · ESC CLOSE`.

## 6. The text (Isao's voice; the owner edits in content)

In `src/content/bestiary.js` as `ENTRIES`: `{ id, name, kind: 'tier' | 'creature', types: [...], stage: true, isao: [lines] }`. The owner's brief: dry, slightly humorous, a science-fiction reference here and there, not overdone. Drafts:

- **white** (the amoeba, the phage): "Small, soft, and in no hurry to be anything else. They come in numbers because one of them is not an argument." / "The amoeba hunts whatever opened the ground. The phage crawls. Both go under the treads with a sound I have stopped finding funny." / "If you see one, you are looking at the wrong one."
- **yellow** (the ghost, the scout): "Fast, light, and convinced it is a fighter pilot. It is not." / "The scout maps you. The ghost just wants to see what happens. Neither has a plan for the treads." / "Rammable. Satisfyingly so."
- **blue** (the saucer, the jellyfish, the slime): "Three bad ideas in one belt: a saucer that weaves, a jellyfish that pulses, and a slime that heals if you let it." / "Do not let it. Ram the slime first, admire the jellyfish later." / "Still soft. Still ours."
- **orange** (the drifter): "The first one with something hard inside. You will notice. The hull will notice more." / "It drifts like it has nowhere to be, then it is in front of you. Shoot it; do not drive at it." / "A pearl, I think. Nobody asked it to be beautiful."
- **green** (the virus, the shellback): "Armoured twice over and slowed by what it takes. The shellback waits at the edge of the sentries' range like it has read the manual." / "It has not read the manual. It has read you." / "Solid core. The Bofors has opinions about it."
- **purple** (the barbed mine, the prime): "The barbed one speeds up when hit, which is the opposite of what I would do. The prime regenerates, which is the opposite of what I can do." / "Hit it harder than it heals. There is no other note." / "Cored. Do not test the hull on it; I have the recording."
- **brown** (the rolling mine): "A boulder with intent. Slows when shot, which is less comfort than it sounds." / "Epic, the tactical display says. The tactical display did not have to drive round it." / "Solid core. Stops the hull dead and keeps the change."
- **black** (the phantom, the thorus): "Optical camouflage, a ghost on the radar, and a boss at the end of it that accelerates when you hurt it." / "The phantom is there when the radar says it is not. Trust the ground, not the screen." / "Cored. If the thorus is in the log, so was a very long night."
- **cutis** (the Cutis-Maku; `stage: true` if the probe's seating transfers, else false): "A hunter that walks on its stalks and thinks with its skin. The lab calls it Cutis-Maku; I call it the reason the gate stays shut." / "It is cheap in hundreds and that is the problem."
- **reed** (the Reeds): "Pulled itself out of a sinkhole by its arms. I watched. I would like to stop watching." / "Small, medium, big. The big one is not small."
- **nih-dairia** (the Nih-Dairia, boss; `stage: false` in this piece): "Thirty metres of it. The gunship has three guns and I have twelve hit points; we have discussed the division of labour." / "Fear works on it. So does SOL. So does leaving."
- `console_first` (one line, on the first pass under the gantry): "There is a terminal under the gantry. It has a bestiary. I wrote most of it; do not read it in my voice."
