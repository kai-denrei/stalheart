🌑🌒🌓🌔🌕🌖🌗🌘. I am in general against using emojis and unicode in UI, but perhaps this is helpful here." "I also want to accelerate the

Isao surprises us during a lull with a creation he made, a Disco Ball! 42 days surviving on this planet, Arthur would have been proud of us!
bring your towel and let's party! cue in retro-synth wave music `~/Downloads/44s_80s-retro-synth-wave.mp3` and a new disco ball asset from

than an emoji, and the party as a short camera cut followed by the party going on in the world while the player plays, ended by the klaxon.

## Decisions

1. **A day is thirty seconds.** `STORY_DAY.seconds` 300 → 30; `dayShare` and `tilt` unchanged (18 s of sun, 12 s of night, dawn and dusk about
   3 s each side of the horizon, as the elevation ramps scale). 80 days in a forty-minute run. The lab rigs follow (they read `STORY_DAY`).

2. **The rig counts days.** `createDaylight` keeps `day` (1 on the first dawn; +1 each time the phase wraps) and reports it in `state()` and
   through a `day()` getter. Dawn is phase 0, so day N begins at phase 0 of its cycle. The `?day=` flag: a value below 1 is a starting phase
   as today; a value of 1 or more is a day number, the run starting at the dawn of that day (`?day=42` is the playtest and harness entry).
   The parsing is a pure function in `src/core/daylight.js` (`dayFlag(raw) → { phase, day }`) so it is tested.

3. **The phase disc replaces the dial.** Same slot (`#day-dial`, top centre beside the sound toggle), 26 px, an inline SVG drawn by
   `makeDial`: a rim circle, a lit disc, and a dark shape whose terminator sweeps continuously with the sun's elevation. Reading: full at noon,
   new at midnight, the shadow on the LEFT through the morning (waxing, the lit part growing from the right) and on the RIGHT through the
   evening (waning), the same convention as the owner's 🌑🌒🌓🌔🌕🌖🌗🌘 row read left to right. The lit fraction is NOT the daylight
   scalar (it saturates at 1 for most of the day and 0 for most of the night, so the terminator would stand still); it is continuous in the
   sun's angle: `f = (1 − cos(sunAngle(p, dayShare))) / 2` (dawn: a half-lit disc, noon full, dusk half-lit, midnight dark), waning when
   `sunAngle mod 2π > π` (after noon, until midnight). Geometry: the terminator is a half-ellipse; for lit fraction `f` the ellipse's
   half-width is `|1 − 2f|·r`, bulging toward the lit side when `f > 0.5` and toward the dark side when `f < 0.5`
   (`src/core/daylight.js` `phaseGlyph(phase, dayShare) → { lit, waning, rx, bulge }` pure; the SVG path built from it in `makeDial`). Colours: the lit fill the HUD's ice blue (`#cfe9ff`), warming toward the golden hour's
   orange by `g` as the dial does today; the dark fill the night's blue-black; the rim as today. Under the disc a small figure, `DAY 42`,
   in the HUD's small caps (one `<b>`), updated when the day changes. CSS stays in styles.css under the existing comment. No emoji anywhere.

4. **DAY 42 is a party, once a run.** A new module `src/fx/disco-party.js` (`createDiscoParty(h)`) is built with the story and torn down with
   it (`restore()` beside `daylight.restore()`), ticked each frame from td-tab's frame (one line beside `hullConsole.tick`), with the host
   handing it: `daylight` (the day), `sectorRun` (the breath), `storyBase` (the Stålheart), `camera`, `startShot`, `showBrief`, `sfx`,
   `shotActive`, `seated()` (any seat or pilot mode), `cardOpen()` (any modal: pause, debrief, briefing, choice card), `scene` and `metres`.
   The party **arms** when `daylight.day() >= DAY_42.day` and the sector loop is fighting and **in a breath**: `sectorRun.state().phase ===
   'fighting'`, the next pulse not yet due and `sectorRun.breathing()` true (a new API entry: for a pulse sector, `lastPulse.sent` and alive
   bodies under `breath.at` of the last size; for a flow sector, alive under a quarter of its target), no shot active, nobody seated, no card
   open, the hull alive. A sector that is already secure (phase `secure` / the debrief) also counts as a lull. If no such moment comes by
   `DAY_42.day + DAY_42.patience` days, the party goes at the next moment with no shot, seat or card, breath or not (the joke must land).

5. **The sequence** (the party's own clock, in seconds from arming; all numbers in `DAY_42`):
   - `0.0` Isao's brief `day_42` (face `glee`, title `COMMS · ISAO`, lines: "Forty-two days surviving on this planet. Arthur would have been
     proud of us.", "Bring your towel. Let's party!"). The sector clock **holds**: td-tab's `frozen` is unchanged; instead `createSectorRun`
     gains `h.clockHeld?.()` and skips its `tick` body (clock, gates, releases) while it returns true; the party returns true from its brief
     to its end, so no pulse comes due and the `stall` timer does not run. Enemies already in the field keep walking and the base keeps
     shooting (the player asked for a lull, not a freeze).
   - `0.0` the music: started through `sfx.loop('day_42_music')` (the only call that returns a handle with `stop(fade)`; `play()` returns
     nothing) and stopped by the party at `DAY_42.seconds` 43.8 s, before the loop point, so it plays **once**; on a new bus `music` (level 0.8 by default, in the audio page's bus list), the `towers`/`tank` buses ducked to 0.6 for
     the track; the klaxon is on `ui`, unducked.
   - `DAY_42.shot.at` (1.5 s, the brief's first words shown) the camera cut `discoReveal`, `DAY_42.shot.seconds` 10 s, **live** (added to
     td-tab's live-shot list, so the world runs under it), skippable by a key or tap like the tours, the pose a slow quarter-orbit at
     `DAY_42.shot.radius` metres (70) and `height` (28) round the Stålheart's `EXTRUSION_TIP` world position, looking at the hanging ball,
     eased in from the camera's current pose over the first 1.2 s (the sites tour's blend), the Stålheart's near tier **forced** (the LOD
     driver already loads it when the eye is near; the shot's eye is near).
   - `DAY_42.hoist` (the shot's first 4 s): the arm goes from its current Terraforming pose to the celebration pose (`actions.Terraforming_Cycle`
     paused, joints lerped by the party's `t / hoist`), the ball appears at the tip (scale 0 → 1 over the first second, hanging), then the
     16 s celebration loop runs: pendulum, sphere spin, halo, laser ring, party floodlights (four `THREE.SpotLight`s in `PARTY_COLORS` hung
     from the ball's halo node, sweeping with `spotPitch`, intensity `pulse`; no shadows) and four thin additive laser quads from the ring
     (`laserPitch`), all driven by the pure state. The floodlights are **the only lights added** and are removed at the end.
   - the shot ends (10 s or skipped): camera back to the player's view (`snapCamera`), play continues; the ball and lights go on, the music
     plays. Isao's floor line in the chat flavour is NOT added (the owner asked him quieter); the party's only words are the two briefs.
   - `DAY_42.seconds` 44 (the track's length, or the music handle's end if the browser reports it first): **the alarm**. The music handle is
     stopped (`fade` 0.15), `sfx.play('danger_alert')`, the callout `ALARM · CONTACT` (`co-victory` class, as the stampede's), the brief
     `day_42_over` (face `focused`, lines: "Party's over. They heard the bass."), the arm lowers the ball over 3 s to rest at the tip's
     Terraforming pose height and the ball **shrinks away** (scale 1 → 0, removed; no cradle, no clutter left in the yard), the Terraforming
     action resumes from where it was paused, the floodlights and lasers are removed, the duck lifted, the clock hold released and the
     next pulse **armed at once** through the board's own lead-in (`armWave()`, src/fx/enemy-step.js: the three-second warn rings, then
     `sectorRun.release()` for every open breach; the same path the stall safety uses), handed to the party by the host as `armWave`;
     if the sector is secure or has no open breach, nothing is armed and the brief alone ends the party. While the party holds, the sector
     run's `pulseGap()` returns `Infinity`, so the board's between-waves clock (td-tab ~5068: `interClock >= gap - WAVE_WARN`) never arms.
   - `done` for the run; `restore()` (NEW RUN) resets to `idle`.
   A pause (P / the pause card) mid-party: the music follows whatever the host does to the ambient bed under the pause card (the party does
   not touch it); the party's clock, like the world, does not advance, because the party is ticked with the frame's `frozen ? 0 : dt`.

6. **Where the numbers live.** `src/content/day-42.js` exports `DAY_42` (frozen): `day: 42, patience: 6, seconds: 44, shot: { at: 1.5,
   seconds: 10, radius: 70, height: 28, easeIn: 1.2 }, hoist: 4, lower: 3, loop: 16, colors: [0xff4fd8, 0x48e5ff, 0xffcf48, 0x68ff8a],
   light: { intensity: 18, angle: 0.5, distance: 60 }, laser: { length: 40, width: 0.18 }, music: { key: 'day_42_music', bus: 'music', gain: 0.8,
   duck: { buses: ['towers', 'tank'], depth: 0.6 } }, briefs: { start: 'day_42', over: 'day_42_over' }, callout: 'ALARM · CONTACT'`. The two
   briefs go in `src/isaobriefs.js` beside the descent's; the two lines are appended to `docs/ISAO-VOICE-LINES.md` as **section 14. Day 42**
   (unrecorded; captions until the owner records them).

7. **The pose is pure.** `src/domain/disco-celebration.js` ports the diorama's `celebrationState(time)` and `discoState(time)` as one function
   `celebrationPose(t) → { gantryZ, carriageX, liftY, j1Yaw, j2Shoulder, j3Elbow, j5Wrist, pendulumZ, pendulumX, sphereSpinY, haloYaw,
   laserRingYaw, laserPitch, spotPitch, pulse }` with the diorama's constants, plus `hoistBlend(k, from, to)` for the lerp from the paused
   Terraforming joints and `HOOK_LOCAL_OFFSET`. Applying it to nodes by name lives in the fx module (`applyPose(stalheartRoot, ballRoot, pose)`),
   which tolerates missing nodes (the far tier is batched: the hoist waits for the near tier, and the ball hangs from the far tier's tip
   position if the near one never arrives).

8. **The asset.** `scripts/import-disco-ball.mjs` (local tool, never in CI) downloads the published LOD1 plain GLB
   (`https://jelaludo.github.io/SentryTowers_A6/assets/disco-ball/lod1/disco_ball_d0_lod1.glb`) and the diorama's `runtime.js`, `layout.json`
   and README into `assets/models/disco-ball/` (`disco_ball_d0_lod1.glb`, `README.md` naming the source, the pin, the licence note "Models by
   jelaludo; the owner's own") and pins URL, bytes and SHA-256 in `docs/disco-ball-assets.lock.json`, failing if the ball's node names
   (`DISCO_BALL_ROOT`, `DISCO_PENDULUM_PIVOT`, `DISCO_SPHERE_SPIN`, `LIGHT_HALO_YAW`, `LASER_RING_YAW`, `STANDALONE_CRADLE`) are not all in
   the GLB's JSON chunk. The ball is loaded through `src/glbmodels.js` (`loadGlbWithClips` or the plain loader there) lazily, **the first time
   the day count reaches `DAY_42.day − 2`**, so the load is off the frame when the party starts. The music is copied to
   `assets/audio/day_42_music.mp3` and added to the audio table as `day_42_music` (bus `music`, gain 0.8, maxVoices 1, minInterval 10); the
   audio manifest test (`test/audiomanifest.mjs`) covers the file's presence as it does the others.

9. **Hooks in td-tab, and nothing more.** (a) the daylight construction reads `dayFlag(flags.day)`; (b) the live-shot regexp gains
   `discoReveal`; (c) `createSectorRun` is handed `clockHeld: () => discoParty?.holding()`; (d) `discoParty` is created after `sectorRun`
   on a story build and restored with the daylight; (e) `discoParty?.tick(dt)` in the frame beside `hullConsole.tick`; (f) the harness state
   gains `day: daylight?.day() ?? null` and `party: discoParty?.state() ?? null` (`{ phase: 'idle'|'armed'|'party'|'over'|'done', t, hanging,
   lights, music }`), and `gameHooks.partyNow()` arms the party at once (harness and dev drawer). (g) the dev drawer's jump row gains
   **DAY 42** (`?skip=defence&sector=4&day=42`). The hooks are one line each; the controller does not grow past its ceiling (the architecture
   guard is the proof; if a hook costs a line, a line is saved beside it).

10. **The host's guards** (owner, 2026-10-16: probes before fixes): the party never arms during the opening (the sector loop is idle then),
    the canyon pass (SOL's seat is a seat), the arena (phase `fighting` with `def.boss`: excluded explicitly, the hull is Isao's there), the
    descent (an iframe over the paused game: a card), or the finale (`player.won`). The brief obeys the one-deep rule (if Isao is mid-line the
    party waits a second and tries again rather than dropping its line).
