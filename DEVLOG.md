## 2026-09-13 — The packed release drew the hull and the drone as broken geometry: integer attributes transformed raw

Owner screenshot of build 249e1147: the tank rendered as a glowing column, the drone broken. Reproduced on the release build served locally: the hull a flat dark polygon while its triangle count still matched.

gltfpack's default quantisation stores positions as 16-bit integers with the dequantising scale on the node; mergeByMaterial cloned such a geometry and applied a matrix straight into the integer array.
